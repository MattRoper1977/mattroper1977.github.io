Welcome to Lumina Haven

Decorate: choose pieces from the bottom catalogue, then drag them anywhere on the floor. Rotate, recolour or delete the selected piece with the tools on the right.

Grow: plants lose water slowly. Use the watering can, let growth reach 100%, then prune an overgrown plant for Garden Essence and a Golden Bloom.

Collect: glowing flowers and gems appear in the room. Tap them, then combine materials in the Fusion Lab to unlock rare décor.

Relax: switch lighting, weather and procedural sound layers. Tap the cat when it appears. Photo Mode exports your arrangement as a PNG.

Reset the haven?

This restores the starter room and collection. Your exported JSON files are not affected.
