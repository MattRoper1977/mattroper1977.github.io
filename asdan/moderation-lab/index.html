Internal verification sampling

Stratified risk-based IQA sampling across candidate risk scores, assessor allocations and ASCV checks.

This tool does not encode ASDAN's published sampling requirements. The stratified method spreads the sample across candidates, risk scores and assessors; it does not claim to cover assessment methods or locations, and the percentages offered here are convenience defaults, not a centre rule. Typed names and date stamps support your workflow — sample size and plan must follow your centre's current IV/EQA arrangements and ASDAN's own guidance.
