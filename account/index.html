What an account changes

Useful, not a paywall

  • Your member favourites and account settings can follow you between devices.
  • Existing public Games, Lessons, Apps, Tools and Resources remain available without an account.
  • Offline-first content keeps working; cloud sync simply waits until a connection returns.
  • Joining the mailing list is a separate choice. Creating an account never subscribes you.

Delete account

Account deletion removes the authentication identity, profile and synchronised member data. It is permanent and cannot be undone.

Mailing-list subscription is deliberately separate and is not removed by deleting your account. Unsubscribe first using Mailing preferences below, or the link in any mailing.

Anything saved by a game or lesson on this device, and anything synced by a seven-word code, is not held against your account and is not reached by this deletion.

If self-service deletion is temporarily unavailable, email contactmadebymatt@gmail.com from the account address and request deletion.
