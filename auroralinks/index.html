How to play

Aim: drag anywhere on the course, or use A/D and the arrow keys. The dotted preview adapts to the selected club.

Swing: hold the large swing button or Space. Release at the power you want. Fade and draw add controlled side spin.

Plan: wind changes every hole. Sand kills speed, rough reduces control, water adds a penalty stroke and returns the ball to its previous safe position. On the green, the cyan arrows point downhill.

Call your score: before each tournament hole, predict your strokes. The separate Call Rating rewards reading and nerve, not only perfect timing.

Course Lab: place a tee, pin, bunkers, water and trees with mouse or touch, then test and export the layout.
