FLIGHT BRIEFING

DESKTOP MOVEMENTUse WASD or the arrow keys. Aim with the mouse. Hold left-click or Space to fire. Shift or right-click performs a vector dash.
MOBILE MOVEMENTLeft stick moves. Right stick aims and fires. Tap DASH to cut through danger. Auto-fire can be enabled from the title screen.
GRAZE & OVERDRIVEPass close to warm-coloured enemy fire without touching it. Grazes increase your multiplier and fill the Overdrive bar.
BUILD SYNERGYAfter every wave, choose one upgrade. Damage, split-shot, fire rate, piercing, chain arcs, homing and dash modifiers multiply together.
WEAPON EVOLUTIONCollect three green Upgrade Cores to evolve: Twin Plasma → Triple Spread → Vector Missiles → Nova Cannon.
THREAT BUDGETWaves are built procedurally from drones, flankers, tanks, snipers, mortars and phasing hunters. Bosses arrive every fifth wave.
SCORINGEach kill raises the multiplier. No kill for 2.5 seconds starts gradual decay. Damage halves the multiplier rather than wiping it out.
ACCESSIBILITYPlayer fire is always cool cyan/green; hazards are warm orange/magenta. Reduced motion disables camera roll, hit-stop and most shake.
