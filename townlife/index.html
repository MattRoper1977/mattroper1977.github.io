How to play

Walk or drive: WASD or arrow keys. Interact: E. Enter or exit vehicle: F. Spawn vehicle: V. Drift: Space. Siren: H. Headlights: L. Horn: X. Build: B. Rotate furniture: R. Remove: right-click or use the Remove Tool.

Walk through marked door thresholds to enter buildings. Roofs dissolve only when the on-foot player is genuinely inside. Furniture such as sofas, beds, lamps and televisions can be used with E outside build mode.

Role jobs retain distinct mechanics: Courier cargo physics, Paramedic pulse timing, Officer sector scans, Builder contracts, Resident errands and Mischief safe cracking.

Gold systems: approach named citizens and press E to talk; open the Town Ledger to manage affinity, businesses, operations, governance and tuning. Claim a home, place powered appliances, cameras and monitors, then open Utilities to select mains, generator or solar power. Build mode includes a Wire Circuits tool that assigns devices to lighting, appliance, security or workshop circuits. CCTV feeds depend on an online breaker and security circuit. Traffic, pedestrians and interceptors use context steering around roadblocks. Mayor decrees change patrols, deed prices, solar performance and commercial income.
