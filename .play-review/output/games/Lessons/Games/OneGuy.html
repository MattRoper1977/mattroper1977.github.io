HOW TO PLAY

🏃 Running

One button. TAP / CLICK / SPACE to jump. Hold to jump higher — release early for a short hop. Spikes and gaps end your run.

👻 The ghost

You race a recording of the last run on this device (or a pasted code). Sprint past the spot where it fell to take the crown — then every metre sets a new bar. Beat a run? Copy your code and send the challenge on.

👹 Boss fights — how to win

Every zone ends in a boss arena. The boss fires orbs at you: some roll along the ground, some bounce.

🦶 Land on TOP of an orb while falling to smash it back at the boss. Every returned orb knocks off 1 ♥ — empty its hearts and the gate opens.

⚠️ Touching an orb from the side is fatal, and so is running into the boss before it’s beaten. Jump over what you can’t stomp!

🎒 Locker & extras

Metres run, ghosts beaten and bosses slain unlock skins and trails in the locker. 🌙 Calm mode softens flashes and screen-shake.
