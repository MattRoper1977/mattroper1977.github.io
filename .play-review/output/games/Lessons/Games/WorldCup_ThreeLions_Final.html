🇦🇷 Round 1 — Argentina's Festival of Flair

The squad on the ball (glowing above) picks a shot, gets a 🗣️ conference, then answers. Miss it and the other squads can STEAL for +1⚽! Possession rotates after every card. Argentina may fire ¡Otra vez! once (retry a miss, reward −1⚽ — tap their gold badge).

If Argentina don't win: "Their flair impressed the officials — they discovered the code while helping the winning team." The real round winner stands.

The squad on the ball answers — and within that squad, only the pupil in possession speaks, then passes to someone who hasn't answered recently. A miss can be STOLEN for +1⚽. Spain may fire Perfect Pass once (tap their gold badge). No back-to-back answers!
