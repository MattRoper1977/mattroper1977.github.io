How to play

One input. Tap the screen or press Space / ↑. What it does depends on your form.

Bastion (hex) — tap to jump. Ring (circle) — tap to invert gravity. Star — hold to rise, release to fall. M — tap to flap.

Shift Gates change your form mid-run. They are announced early with a shape and a word — never colour alone.

The pulse is the beat. Every hazard lands on it. Watch the ring from the Mark and pre-empt.

You do not lose. Touch a hazard and you smudge — a rewind to the last checkpoint. Your furthest progress stays.
