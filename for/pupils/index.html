Emberwild

An original creature-collector. Explore the Vale, battle and capture Emberkin, then follow a seam in the rock down into procedurally generated Depths — cavern, ruins, then hybrid — with loot, healing pools and a Depthwarden on the last floor. Before every throw you call your own odds, and the game then shows you the real ones. Fainting costs you the floor, never the run. One file, no install, plays offline.

Matt’s take: Madebymatt meets creature collecting - shh, you know the one.
