Made by Matt boundaries

Capability without unsupported claims

Use these as discovery routes for your own planning, not as claims about outcomes, attainment or approval by any awarding body or school. An adult or teacher account is optional and supports account-backed features such as carrying saved Made by Matt shortcuts between devices. Teacher updates use a separate double-opt-in mailing list; creating an account never subscribes you.
