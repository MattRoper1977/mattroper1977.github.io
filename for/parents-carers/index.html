Questions families often ask

Parent and carer FAQ

Does a child need an account to use public games or lessons?

No. Public games, lessons and learning areas can be opened without creating an account.

What does the pupil homepage change?

It changes presentation and navigation only. It does not create a child profile, grant permissions or hide other public content.

Is the mailing list connected to an account?

No. Teacher updates use a separate adult mailing-list process, and creating an account never subscribes anyone.

Does Made by Matt replace school or safeguarding advice?

No. Made by Matt provides learning content and tools. Official external material is labelled by publisher in the Education Hub.
