UPDATED · Neon Sync v1.1 — a fourth hero and a second way to play.

  • Volt joins the roster as a Striker. Tether Darts chain between enemies, Pin Bolt locks a target down, and Surge Rail lays a lane that speeds up whoever runs it — your team included.
  • Escort Rush is a whole second mode. Attackers push the Sound Float along one route with one checkpoint; defenders hold it until the five-minute horn. Overtime only survives while attackers are actually pushing.
  • The score is still not your aim — it is whether you were the teammate everyone wants. Hold the objective, back your squad, get endorsed. A support who never eliminates anyone still outscores a lone striker.

NEW · Neon Breach — hold the arena, wave after wave.

  • Survive escalating drone waves in a procedural neon arena. Clear a wave, draft an upgrade, go again — the run is over when you are, and every run is built differently.

Recently improved

July 2026 · Tutor Time

All ten Tutor Time decks rebuilt on one engine

  • An illustration on every deck. Ten hand-drawn scenes that argue the idea rather than decorate it — scales that don't bend, five routes on one hand, a mend drawn stronger than the break.
  • Phone-feed and news-band scenes on Deepfakes, Vapes and Pressure Points — a group chat going from a joke to a threat in ten minutes, with the timestamps showing.
  • Calm Mode on every deck. One tap stills every animation and silences the chime. Everything also respects the reduced-motion setting on the device.
Three more improvements
  • An anonymous box that stays private. What a pupil types never appears on the screen and is never saved to the device — it reaches staff on a printed sheet with a safeguarding sign-off, and nowhere else.
  • Class lists clear themselves. Saved on the device as before, but wiped automatically after a fortnight, with a clear-now button in the cold-call panel.
  • Progress seals and a printable evidence sheet on every deck, so a session leaves a record without anyone writing one up.

THE PROMISE | how every tool behaves

Free

Everything here is free to use — no trials, no locked features, nothing to sign up for. There is no account, so there is nothing to forget the password to.

Nothing uploaded

Lessons, registers and pupil records stay in your browser on your device — nothing about your class is ever sent anywhere. Three things do reach the internet. One happens on its own — an anonymous visit counter, on this page and the stats page. Two happen only if you choose them: sending the contact form, and pressing play on a video. All four, in plain English →

Offline-friendly

Core experiences remain useful where connectivity is unreliable.

Teacher-built

Designed in a real classroom — accessible, calm-mode aware and ready for the whiteboard.

Say hello

Questions, ideas, bug reports, or just tell me what would be useful. When you press Send, your name, email and message are posted to formsubmit.co — a third-party relay I do not run. I can tell you what your browser sends and where it goes; what happens after that is theirs, not mine. What this site does with data →

Or skip the relay entirely and email me directly at contactmadebymatt@gmail.com — same inbox, nothing in between.

Teacher updates

For teachers and other adults. An occasional email when a new lesson pack, tool or game goes up. That is all it is for — no newsletter, no schedule I would have to keep, and nothing automated.

This is separate from an account. Making an account does not put you on this list, and joining this list does not make you an account. Accounts never leave your own device; this list means sending an email address to the same third-party relay as the contact form, so only do it if you are happy with that.

Under 18? Please don't use this form — ask a teacher to sign up instead. Everything on the site is free without it.
