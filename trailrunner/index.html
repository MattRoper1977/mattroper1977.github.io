WHAT CHANGED IN MILESTONE 2

            
A real Catmull–Rom corridor now supplies stable tangent/side/up frames for climbing, descending and banking trail segments. Steering remains three-lane lateral play; this build does not claim loops, wall-runs or unrestricted 6DOF.

            
High graphics adds bounded shader rain, atmospheric storm fog and velocity-fed radial warp/chromatic separation. Calm Mode, reduced motion, Low graphics and the visual-only governor reduce those effects without changing simulation difficulty.

            
Storm bosses now telegraph lane strikes before releasing a chase wave. Clear five consecutive legs in one run without losing energy to unlock the Stormbreak Capacitor in the shared local Passport; any energy loss resets that streak.
