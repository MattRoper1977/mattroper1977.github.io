Controls

Desktop: W/↑ throttle, S/↓ brake and reverse, A/D or ←/→ steer, Space handbrake, Z rewind, C camera, Esc pause. Gamepads use the left stick, triggers, A and X.

Touch: use the steering pad and hold the labelled pedals. Portrait mode reserves a dedicated control tray so the road stays visible. All controls are at least 44px.

V6 guidance: the projected racing line, braking call and corner confidence display are presentation aids only; they never steer the car or alter stage timing.

V6 stores only local records, progression, works-garage upgrades, daily results, settings, rally-event summaries, local ghost telemetry and its local schema-6 profile. It makes no network requests.
