Controls

Desktop: W/↑ throttle, S/↓ brake and reverse, A/D or ←/→ steer, Space handbrake, Z rewind, C camera, Esc pause. Gamepads use the left stick, triggers, A and X.

Touch: drag the steering pad and hold the labelled pedals. All controls are at least 48px. Voice calls, sound, camera movement and driving assists can be changed in the garage.

The game stores only local records, progression, settings, rally-event progress summaries and local ghost telemetry. It makes no network requests.
