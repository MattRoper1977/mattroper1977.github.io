In every lesson: the teach dock

Open any current lesson and tap the TEACH pill at the bottom of the screen. A floating dock brings the classroom essentials to you, with no tab-switching and no losing the room.

  • Quick Timer

    1, 5 or 10 minutes overlaid on the corner of your slides. No jarring alarm, just a gentle pulse at zero.

  • Name Picker

    Paste your class list once. It follows you into every lesson and won't repeat until everyone has had a turn.

  • Noise Meter

    A live volume visualiser using this device's mic only. Nothing is ever recorded or uploaded.

  • Calm Reset

    One tap replaces the screen with a slow breathing guide. Connection before correction, then tap to return.
