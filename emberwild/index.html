EMBERWILD

Explore the Vale, battle and capture Emberkin, then follow a seam in the rock down into the Depths. Call your own odds before every throw — and find out what they really were.

All creatures, names, environments, sounds and artwork in this package are original. It contains no Pokémon assets or proprietary franchise content.

WASD / Arrows · Z/Enter confirm · X/Esc back · M menu · T test encounter · P profiler Software 2.5D ready

Field controls & architecture

Explore

Use WASD, arrow keys or the touch D-pad. Movement snaps to grid centres but animates smoothly. Hold Shift to sprint. Approach a character and press Z, Enter, Space or A.

Battle

Choose Fight, Bag, Team or Run. Damage uses derived stats, move priority, speed, STAB, type multipliers, critical rolls and integer truncation. Prism Pods can capture wild Emberkin.

World systems

The route includes elevation, stairs, a directional ledge vault, tall-grass encounters, a water garden, a cutaway lodge, an NPC sightcone and an elevation-aware A* pathfinder.

Rendering

The built-in low-resolution software renderer is fully offline. When Three.js r128 is reachable, the game upgrades itself to an orthographic WebGL scene with billboard sprites, nearest filtering and dithered roof occlusion.

Audio & accessibility

Every sound is synthesized with Web Audio pulse, noise and FM channels. Audio unlocks only after a user gesture. High contrast, screen-reader status mirroring, keyboard access and reduced-motion browser preferences are supported.

Testing tools

Press T to trigger a safe test encounter and P to show performance telemetry. Press I to import a JSON map exported by the companion editor.
