Come home to a wounded Vale. Learn why frightened Emberkin are reclaiming the land, uncover the human choices that awakened the Reclamation Pulse, and decide what kind of world both sides will inherit.

Field controls & architecture

Explore

Use WASD, arrow keys or the touch D-pad. Movement snaps to grid centres but animates smoothly. Hold Shift to sprint. Approach a character and press Z, Enter or Space—or tap the on-screen A button.

Battle

Early Vale encounters are deliberately gentler than your level-six starter, and every battle menu is untimed. Damaging techniques offer an optional Resonance Pulse: press A, Z, Enter or Space as the rings meet for bonus power, or ignore it with no penalty. Calm Motion supplies an automatic GOOD pulse. The first encounter explains each choice.

Home & direction

Wayfinder's Rest is your home in the south-west of Wayfinder's Reach. Ena's clinic lies west of the plaza, Corin's Depot to the south-east, and the Water Garden to the north-west. Use your hearth to sleep, revive and fully recharge every Emberkin; the Field Note always gives your next clear destination.

Story & choice

Follow the Reclamation Pulse from Prism Vale and the Depths through Cobalt Estuary, Sunken Canopy, Basalt Caldera, Crystalline Chasm and Aether Peak. Listen to Emberkin, Rootwardens and the Meridian Company, then choose one of five futures at the summit.

World systems

The six-region route includes elevation, stairs, a directional ledge vault, tall-grass encounters, tidal bridges, crystal crossings, a cutaway home, NPC sight cones and an elevation-aware A* pathfinder.

Rendering

Ascension draws the Vale as a true perspective WebGL2 diorama with 3D terrain, cliffs, water, foliage, characters, cinematic lighting and depth fog. It remains completely offline and automatically falls back to the proven software renderer when WebGL2 is unavailable.

Audio, access & privacy

Every sound is synthesized in the browser. Calm motion, high contrast, screen-reader status and full keyboard access are supported. Progress stays on this device only; clearing this browser's site data removes it.

Developer tools

Add ?dev=1 to expose the profiler, safe test encounter and JSON map importer.
